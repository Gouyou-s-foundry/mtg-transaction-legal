MTG Transaction Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

MTG Transaction is a local-first companion app for Magic: The Gathering players. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends to third-party services, and what choices users have.

Information stored on the device

MTG Transaction does not require an account and does not operate a cloud backend. The app stores the following information locally on the user's device:

  • local deck names, categories, and optional descriptions;
  • imported public-deck references from Moxfield or Archidekt;
  • cards in the Wishlist, Watchlist, and Sell list;
  • cached Scryfall card information and public image URLs;
  • fetched price offers, availability, and price history;
  • application preferences and recent application error logs.

This information remains in the app's local SQLite database until the user removes it, clears the app's data, uninstalls the app, or resets the device. MTG Transaction does not sync this local data to its own servers. Application error logs are retained locally for a limited period and periodically cleaned up.

Camera and card photos

The scanner requests camera access only when the user opens the scanner and chooses to allow it. A captured card photo is used on the device for identification and on-device OCR. MTG Transaction does not upload the photo to its own server or send the photo to Scryfall. The selected card name or identifier may then be sent to Scryfall to search for card data.

The app does not request location, health, Motion & Fitness, or microphone access, and does not record video or audio.

Third-party services

When the user uses a feature that needs external data, the app can contact:

  • Scryfall, for card search, card data, and public card-image URLs;
  • Moxfield or Archidekt, for read-only public deck/profile pages;
  • supported retailers, for publicly displayed card prices, availability, and buylist information.

These services may receive normal technical request information such as an IP address and request metadata under their own privacy policies. A price lookup sends the card name needed to search the retailer; MTG Transaction does not send a user account or payment information to a retailer. Public provider pages may also use cookies or their own web-storage rules inside the provider's WebView.

MTG Transaction does not log in to providers, request provider passwords, or write to external decklists. Provider data can change and should be validated on the provider's website.

Email export

The optional wishlist export opens the user's installed Gmail or email composer with a draft. The user decides whether to edit, address, and send the message. MTG Transaction does not send the email itself and does not receive a copy of it.

Analytics, advertising, and payments

The current version does not include an MTG Transaction analytics system, advertising SDK, or remote crash-reporting service. Future versions may include analytics, advertising, diagnostics, and optional location-based features. If introduced, these services may collect limited information such as search history, product interaction and usage data, advertising data, approximate location, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data to understand popular cards, suggest nearby shops when permission is granted, improve the app, and measure its performance. Third-party advertising providers may also process technical or usage information needed to display and measure ads, in accordance with their own policies and any choices or permissions available in the app or operating system. This policy will be updated before these features are introduced.

The app offers optional Tip Jar contributions through Apple's in-app purchase system. Tips are voluntary, do not unlock features, and are processed by Apple through the App Store. MTG Transaction does not receive or store the user's payment card details, billing address, or other payment credentials.

Children's privacy

MTG Transaction is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and deletion

Local data is protected by the device's application sandbox and the user's device security. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. To delete local MTG Transaction data, delete the app or use the operating system's available app-data controls.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with material revisions.

Privacy questions can be sent to gouyousfoundry@gmail.com.

MTG Transaction is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Wizards of the Coast, Scryfall, Moxfield, Archidekt, or any listed retailer.
